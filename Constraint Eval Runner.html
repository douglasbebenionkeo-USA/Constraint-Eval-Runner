Constraint Eval Runner

Paste a model's poem under each prompt. Constraints are checked automatically; you mark topic and coherence. Score two model versions to see hill climbing in action.

How scoring works
  • Each prompt earns 1 point per explicit constraint met, plus 1 for topic and 1 for coherence.
  • Prompt score = points earned ÷ points possible. Overall score = average of scored prompts.
  • Strict pass = every point earned on a prompt. Strict pass rate = share of scored prompts that are strict passes.
  • Lines are counted by line breaks. For prompt 10 the first line is treated as the title.
  • Rhyme checks are a heuristic (matching last two letters of the end words). Click a rhyme check to override it.
  • Keep prompts and rubric fixed between runs so a higher score means a better model, not an easier test.
